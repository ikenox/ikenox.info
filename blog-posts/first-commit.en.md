I tried building a blog with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb-ja.github.io/) + [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/).

- You can get a blog published in about 10 minutes, and it still leaves plenty of room for customization afterward
- You can write posts in Markdown
- You can version-control your posts with Git

There's a lot to like about it.
My day-to-day learning tends to lean toward input, so I'd like to make use of this blog as a place for output.<br>
By the way, for the Jekyll theme I started from [Tale](https://github.com/chesterhow/tale/).
